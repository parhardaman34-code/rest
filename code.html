Where Garden Aesthetics Meet
Vibrant Multi-Cuisine Dining

Nestled at 74-D, D-Block in Sarabha Nagar (tucked peacefully behind Kips Market), Garden Cafe is Ludhiana's destination for lovers of greenery, artisanal coffees, and comforting global flavors. Designed as a verdant escape from city bustle, our cafe pairs nature-drenched outdoor garden seating with a chic, comfortable air-conditioned indoor lounge.

Whether you are craving slow-steeped hazelnut iced brews, gourmet Italian wood-fired pizzas, creamy Alfredo pastas, cheesy Mexican nachos, or sizzling Indo-Asian stir-fries — every plate is prepared fresh with hand-selected ingredients and genuine warmth.
