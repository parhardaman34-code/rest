Where Aristocratic Heritage Meets
Refined Culinary Artistry

Nestled along Madhav Rao Scindia Marg in City Center, Raksha Vihar, Alfanzo stands as a living homage to Gwalior's celebrated regal indulgence. We resurrect long-guarded Awadhi spice recipes, slow charcoal infusions, and grand Mughlai culinary treatises inside an ambient sanctuary of dark velvet banquettes, antique brass candelabras, and crystal chandeliers.

Every handi is prepared without shortcuts — sealed earthen pots (dum), fragrant saffron infusions, and hand-ground spice masalas that honor centuries of Central Indian and Nawabi dining prestige.
