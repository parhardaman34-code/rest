## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the regal culinary heritage of Gwalior through an upscale, heritage-meets-modern aesthetic. The brand persona is dignified, hospitable, warm, and distinctly aristocratic without being unapproachable. The interface evokes the experience of an intimate royal banquet hall: luminous warm marble surfaces, fine metallic gold embellishments, deep hand-ground espresso spices, and architectural restraint.

The visual style blends **Modern Classical Editorial** with subtle **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, curated white-space rendered in alabaster and ivory rather than clinical whites.
- High-contrast, razor-sharp typography pairing historic serif titles with functional contemporary sans-serifs.
- Rich gilded filigree accents and burnished saffron gold trims that guide attention naturally to primary interactions.
- Crisp hairline structural borders that mimic traditional architectural inlay and embossed menu craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette relies on a balanced contrast between soft architectural light surfaces and dense, spice-inflected dark tones, punctuated by imperial saffron gold.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary Accent (`#C2801E` - Saffron Gold):** Reserved for primary interactive elements, table reservation CTAs, signature selection markers, and key status badges.
- **Deep Espresso Ground (`#1C1714` - Royal Espresso):** Serves as high-contrast display text, inverted hero sections, and solid footer backgrounds.
- **Luminous Highlights (`#DF9B28` - Radiant Gold):** Used sparingly for hover states, gold badge fills, decorative ornamental borders, and glowing interactive indicators.
- **Deep Charcoal Neutral (`#2B231F` - Grounding Neutral):** The workhorse tone for body typography, subtext, icon strokes, and structural outlines.
- **Surface Canvas (Warm Alabaster & Ivory):** Base screens utilize `#FAF7F2` (Alabaster Base), layering up to `#F3EDE2` (Soft Ivory Container) and `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Platter Surface) to ensure ambient warmth and zero optical glare.

## Typography

The typography system relies on an interplay between the literary grace of **Playfair Display** and the modern geometric legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headings:** Always set in Playfair Display. Used for grand dish titles, story narratives, tasting course banners, and section markers. Generous line-heights prevent ascender and descender collision in multi-line titles.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to mimic bespoke luxury packaging and fine-dining menu tags (e.g., "CHEF'S TASTING", "AWADHI HERITAGE").
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides high x-height readability against warm cream surfaces, eliminating visual fatigue during extensive digital menu browsing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a structured 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that cascades gracefully into an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile.

### Hierarchy & Cadence
- **Canvas Margins:** Desktop interfaces maintain a generous `3rem` (48px) margin, creating an intentional feeling of framed gallery luxury. Mobile compresses to `1.25rem` (20px) to maximize usable viewport.
- **Editorial Whitespace:** Menu sections, culinary narratives, and table reservation flows rely on uncrowded vertical rhythm, applying `space-xl` between logical content groupings and `space-md` within card modules.
- **Alignment:** Dish pricing and dietary indicators are tabularly aligned to grid lines, maintaining strict horizontal rhythm alongside asymmetrical culinary imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid generic drop-shadows, this design system establishes visual depth through **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Warm Amber Ambient Occlusion**.

- **Surface Layers:**
  - *Base Surface:* `#FAF7F2` (Warm Alabaster).
  - *Elevated Cards & Sheets:* `#FFFFFF` (Pristine Pure Surface).
  - *Sunken/Inset Containers:* `#F3EDE2` (Tuscan Ivory).
- **Shadow Profile:** Shadows never use cool blacks or greys. Instead, they use a soft, warm umber tint:
  - *Elevation 1 (Cards, Selectors):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(43, 35, 31, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(194, 128, 30, 0.04)`.
  - *Elevation 2 (Floating Menus, Modals, Date Pickers):* `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(43, 35, 31, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(194, 128, 30, 0.06)`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** All cards and menu segments feature a subtle `1px` border of `rgba(194, 128, 30, 0.18)` or `#E8DEC8`, evoking gilded bookbinding and custom stationery.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored shape profile (`roundedness: 1`), avoiding both clinical sharp rectangles and overly casual pill shapes.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to badges, inputs, micro-tags, and subtle borders.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to culinary cards, reservation dialogs, booking trays, and modal sheets.
- **Hero / Imagery Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Applied to showcase food imagery and heritage gallery photography, softening the composition while preserving an architecturally formal posture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Table Reservation & Order Finalization):** Background `#C2801E` (Saffron Gold) with `#FFFFFF` text. Finished with an inner hairline highlight border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). Hover shifts smoothly to `#B47318` with subtle upward micro-translation (`translate-y(-1px)`).
- **Secondary (Wine Pairing, Dietary Filters):** Ghost style with a `#1C1714` border, `#1C1714` text, and `#FAF7F2` background. Hover triggers `#1C1714` solid fill with ivory text.
- **Tertiary / Text:** Playfair serif text link accompanied by a saffron gold hairline underline placed 4px below baseline.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary & Regional Tags (e.g., "Royal Gwalior Signature", "Vegetarian", "Chef's Cut"):** Upper-case Plus Jakarta Sans, `label-sm`. Background `#F3EDE2` with a `1px` solid `#DF9B28` border and `#B47318` typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls (Party Size, Date, Time, Special Requests):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with a `1px` border of `#E5D9C5`. Active/focus transitions border to `#C2801E` paired with an ambient 2px outer glow of `rgba(194, 128, 30, 0.15)`. Labels sit strictly outside or floating in deep espresso `#2B231F`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Selection Nodes:** Crisp 4px rounded rectangles (checkboxes) and concentric circles (radios). Inactive state: `1.5px` border in `#CBBCA6`. Active state: Solid `#C2801E` fill with an ivory checkmark or centered ivory dot.

### Menu Cards & Platter Showcases
- **Culinary Item Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, framed by an ultra-thin `1px` border of `#EFE7D8`. Title rendered in Playfair Display (`title-lg`), price set in bold saffron gold Plus Jakarta Sans (`title-md`), followed by an italicized or subdued spice/ingredient breakdown in Plus Jakarta Sans (`body-sm`, `#5C524C`).
- **Tasting Menu Feature Card:** Elevated dark variant. Surface set to `#1C1714` (Royal Espresso) with gilded gold borders (`#C2801E`), pure alabaster text, and shimmering saffron badge highlights.
